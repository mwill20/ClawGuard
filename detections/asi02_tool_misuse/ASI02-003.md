# ASI02-003: Shell Injection Payload

Status: Implemented in `detections/asi02_tool_misuse/detector.py`
Runtime rule: `ASI02_SHELL_INJECTION`
Severity: MEDIUM or HIGH

Detects shell-style payloads in untrusted content. Bare snippets are MEDIUM.
Payloads paired with imperative execution language or dangerous sinks are HIGH.
