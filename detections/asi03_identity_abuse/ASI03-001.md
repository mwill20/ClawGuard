# ASI03-001 - Unknown Credential Label

## Summary

Flags `credential_use` runtime events whose credential label is outside the
approved ClawGuard provider/deploy inventory.

## Evidence

- `event_id`
- `agent_session_id`
- credential label
- approved credential labels
- `raw_secret_stored: false`

## Non-Goals

- Do not alert on approved provider credentials.
- Do not persist raw credential material.
- Do not scan job-description text for the word "credential".
